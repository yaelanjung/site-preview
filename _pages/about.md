Hi! I am a postdoctoral researcher working with [Daniel Dilks](https://secure.web.emory.edu/psychology/dilks/main.php) in the Department of Psychology at Emory University.

I study **how the human brain represents places—how these systems are organized, where that organization comes from, and how it develops**.

My work focuses on the neural systems that support <span style="color: #476fb5;">place recognition</span> and <span style="color: #476fb5;">navigation</span>, spanning questions about functional organization in adults, developmental change across childhood, and the origins of this organization in the newborn brain. I use neuroimaging, behavioral methods, and analyses of brain connectivity and representation to understand how the mind builds and uses knowledge about the places around us.
